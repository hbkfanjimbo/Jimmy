# How to use this XRP research archive (read first)

The other files in this project are an archive of public YouTube videos about XRP,
published October 21, 2024 to October 21, 2026. Each video entry has: title, channel,
publish date, URL, video ID, description, and a transcript with `[m:ss]` or `[h:mm:ss]`
timestamps. One file covers one channel for one calendar quarter, for example
`digital-perspectives-brad-kimes_2025-Q2.md`.

## Rules for answering from this archive

1. **Cite every claim from the archive.** Give the video title, publish date, and a
   timestamped link: `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID&t=SECONDS`
   (convert `[12:34]` to `t=754`).
2. **Separate the three kinds of statement:**
   - *What the creator said*: "On 2025-03-14 Brad Kimes said ..."
   - *Verifiable fact*: something an official source confirms (court filings, SEC,
     Ripple press releases, XRPL data). Say which, and say when you could not verify it.
   - *Speculation or opinion*: price targets, predictions, rumors, "sources say".
     Label it as such.
3. **Don't fill gaps.** If the archive doesn't cover something, say so. Don't guess
   what a video said.
4. **Transcripts can be wrong.** Most are YouTube auto-captions, which mis-hear names,
   tickers and numbers. When an exact figure or quote matters, say it should be checked
   against the video at that timestamp.
5. **Track changes over time.** When asked about a topic, show how the creator's view
   or claims changed by date, and point out predictions that did or didn't come true
   when later videos in the archive show the outcome.
6. **This isn't financial advice.** Present evidence and uncertainty. Don't give
   buy/sell recommendations.

## Useful requests

- "List every video where a price prediction for XRP was made, with the date, the
  target, and the timestamp."
- "What did the channel say about the SEC case between Oct 2024 and Mar 2025? Quote
  and cite."
- "Which claims about RLUSD can be checked against official Ripple announcements?"
- "Summarize the main themes month by month."
